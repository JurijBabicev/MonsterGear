Monster Gear Kids Garage

Monster Gear is an open-source educational project for children's toy cars and RC models. Currently under active development as a final submission for Harvard Computer Science (CS50x), it is dedicated entirely to helping any kid showcase their ultimate dream garage.

Proudly sponsored by CornerBit Ltd, who kindly provided the domain and infrastructure, with special thanks to Professor David J. Malan and the CS50 team for the knowledge used to build this project.
